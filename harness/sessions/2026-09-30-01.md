- **The setting is the account's.** A `settings` table on the UserDO (user
  schema 3, `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`, no upgrade step). `POST
  /api/settings` writes it, same-origin only. `/api/me` returns it through a
  new `UserDO.launch`, which stamps the visit and reads the setting in **one
  object call**. No WebSocket message was added inbound.
- **Never chosen is not metric.** The phone asks the browser's locale
  (`unitsForLocale`: region US, LR or MM is US). Nothing is stored until the
  player picks, on the account screen ("Units", two buttons, and a status
  line: saved, pending, or picked from the language setting).
- **The phone caches it** (`client/units.ts`, `satchess.units`) as it caches
  identity: words, never a door. An offline choice is shown at once and kept
  `pending`; the next launch with a connection sends it before believing the
  account's older answer. Last delivered wins, which 0049 accepts.
  `forgetAccount` empties the cache. Pushes are sent one at a time, in order.
- **One formatter**, `src/shared/units.ts`: feet for short things (reach, a
  square, accuracy, a walk to a square), yards for a board, yards then miles
  walked. Metric output is what the screens said before, except that 999.6 m
  now reads "1.0 km" rather than "1000 m". Null reads "not measured".
- **Every distance on screen goes through it**: home, calibration, the field
  screens, create, the board and game readouts, the handshake lines, the
  review, the record, and the GPS "coarse fix" help ("about half a mile").
- **Server refusals travel as figures.** Accuracy, out of reach, back rank,
  and implausible carry send a `Refusal` beside the metric `message`; the
  phone rebuilds the sentence with the same `refusalWords`, and shows
  `message` for anything it does not recognize. The server's metric text is
  unchanged word for word.
- **Advice is round in the player's units**: "15 ft or more", "at least 80 ft
  corner to corner", and a new hint before the first calibration tap, squares
  of "5 to 10 yards" on ground "40 to 80 yards a side". The board is still
  fitted in meters.
- **Left metric on purpose**: the PGN (decision 0041), the simulator panel

## What was learned

- **Headless Chromium is `en-US`.** With a locale default, every driver that
  reads meters off the screen would have read feet. Five of them now pin
  `locale: 'en-GB'` (check-calibrate, -invite, -record, -resume, -review).
- **A driver can tap its own plate** (decision 0048). The simulator emits
  once a second, so the dot can still be a step short of the square when the
  readout flips, and the plate up and to the right of it then covers the
  destination. `check-units` waits for one more fix after each walk. The
  other game drivers have the same exposure; none failed this session.
- **`check-invite` and `check-calibrate` take `--origin`, not `--base`.**
  STATE.md now says so.
