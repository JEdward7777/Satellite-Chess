One new decision, **0053**: the card draws **carries, not walks**.

- **What is drawn.** "Share a picture of this game", folded shut on the
  review screen, works for live and archived games and offline once the
  review is loaded. It draws a 1080 × 1350 card (4:5, so the square board
  gets the full width) with the final position on the replay's unit board
  (no north, no map, no scale). Each carry is a straight line from its lift
  fix to its place fix, both players', in each player's ink, with a ring at
  the lift and a disc at the place. Those two points per move are already
  in the PGN, so the card discloses nothing a shared game file does not.
- **Long games.** The last four carries are bold. Earlier ones are faint
  and smaller, and every line thins as the count grows (`carryEmphasis`).
  Lines are clipped to the board plus the renderer's padding.
- **No walks.** Not the full walk, not the sharer's own, and not a
  coarsened one: 0018 rule 3 and 0052 keep the per-game track with the two
  players. The card never reads `report.tracks`.
- **The figures** are the sharer's own distance walked and longest carry,
  plus the result in their voice ("Won as Black — checkmate"), the move
  count (the last move's number) and the board size, in their units. Each
  is the review screen's own figure. The opponent's distance is not shown.
- **What is left out.** No date, no join code and no names. The file is
  `satellite-chess.png`. The field's name is opt-in: a box that is off
  every time the card opens, offered only for a name a player typed (never
  "My field" or "Shared field"), and never in the text sent beside the
  picture.
- **Seat only.** The card is offered only to a seat, for a game with a
  result. `cardOffered` and `shareCard` both refuse a reader with no seat.
- **Push only.** The PNG is made when the fold opens, because `toBlob` is
  asynchronous and the Share tap must await nothing. It is rebuilt keeping
  only `IHDR`, `PLTE`, `tRNS`, `gAMA`, `sRGB`, `IDAT` and `IEND`. Share goes
  to the sheet with the file, else a page download, else a long press on
  the picture shown on screen. No request, no server copy, no URL.
- **Code.** `client/share-card.ts` (new); `drawCarries` and `carryEmphasis`
  in `client/replay-draw.ts`; `shareImage` in `client/share.ts`; the fold
  in `views/review.ts`; and an optional `pixelRatio` on `drawBoard`, so the
  picture is the same size on every phone. `DEFAULT_FIELD_NAME` moved to
  `client/fields.ts` beside `SHARED_FIELD_NAME`.
- **Privacy statement.** A bullet says what the picture shows and does not.
- **`scripts/check-share.mjs`** plays Fool's mate on a named field, then:
  - opens the card from the review, on the live game for Black and the
    archived one for White, offline, with `navigator.share` recorded;
  - checks every figure against the review, and the name box off by
    default, on when ticked and off again on the next open;
  - checks the discs at the report's own fixes;
  - checks the PNG is 1080 × 1350 with only picture chunks, and has no
    code, name or coordinate in its bytes;
  - checks zero requests while building and sharing, no URL in the share,
    and the download rung.
- **Tests: 1300 → 1328.** `test/share-card.test.ts` sweeps every figure
  against the review in both units. It also sweeps what is left out (the
  tracks, the date, the code, the name), the PNG cleaner, the ladder and
  the emphasis rule.

## What was learned

- Chromium's canvas PNG is already only `IHDR`, `IDAT` and `IEND`, so the
  cleaner removes nothing there. It is a guard for other encoders. Safari
  is untested (`8.5.7`).
- A detached canvas has no CSS size, so `drawBoard` read its backing size
  as its CSS size and multiplied by the device's pixel ratio. That is why
  `pixelRatio` is now an option.
- `document.body.append` does not type-check under `tsconfig.tools.json`
  (the merged DOM and Workers types); use `appendChild`.
