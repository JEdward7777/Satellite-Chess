## What was done

Phase 2 of a four-phase pipeline run (`harness/pipeline/ratchet.md`): O-33
(done in `2026-09-24-02`), then **O-31**, then **O-30**, then **pinch zoom**.
One commit carries this phase. New decision **0044**: the clock never shows
more time than the player has.

- **Cause found and reproduced.** While a place waited for the server, the
  mover's clock jumped *up* to the balance their turn began with. The
  prediction (`client/optimistic.ts`) nulled `startedAt` without banking the
  think time, and the server banks it only when the move lands. On one bar the
  wait is seconds. On a first move that balance is exactly the time control. In
  `wrangler dev`, with the server's answer held for 2 s, the screen went
  29:55 → 30:00 → 30:15. It hits whoever is moving, on either seat.
- **Fix (`10.6.1`):** a predicted place now banks the mover's think at the tap,
  at the tap's estimated server time (`freeze` from `shared/clock.ts` and
  `estimateServerNow`). The increment is not predicted: it arrives with the
  server's answer, as the one allowed rise.
- **Formatter (`10.6.2`):** `formatClock` already rounded mm:ss down. The
  tenths under ten seconds rounded to nearest (9.96 s read "10.0") and now
  truncate. Decision 0044 records "always round down" and rejects rounding up.
- **Debug readout (`10.6.3`, `client/clock-debug.ts`):** off by default.
  Tapping either clock switches it on or off, and the choice is kept on the
  phone across reloads. It shows the server's raw balances, whose turn it is,
  the start instant, `serverNow`, the offset and the snapshot's age, beside
  what the screen shows. It reads the undecorated connection (not the
  prediction) and sends nothing.
- **O-24 fixed:** `check-clock` and the nine drivers with the identical line
  now split `--base` on the first `=`, so `--base=…/?sim=1` works everywhere.
  Moved to `resolved.md`.
- **Tests:** predicted-place clock tests, formatter tests, readout tests, and a
  DO test that the balance on the wire after a move is exact to the
  millisecond. 1021 → **1035**. `check-clock.mjs` gained step 5 (a place held

- **Drivers**, on `wrangler dev` :8831 from a fresh `--persist-to` each time:

## What was learned

- **A prediction that stops a clock must bank it.** `startedAt: null` means
  "show the banked balance", and the banked balance is from the start of the
  turn. The old test asserted the buggy value (`mineMs` 600,000 after three
  minutes of thought) and read as correct, because it was written about the
  *opponent's* clock.
- **Everything the report could have meant on the server side was sound.**
  Time-control setup, `initial_ms`, the offset, reconstruction on wake, and
  pause and resume were all ruled out by reading and tests (listed in O-31).
- **Holding inbound frames in a driver is what makes an in-flight state
  visible.** Locally the round trip is milliseconds, and the bug could not be
  seen at all.
