## What was learned

What each review round found, in plain terms:

- **Round 1.** A creator waiting on e1 of their whole field kept the "on the
  back rank" flag when a joiner with a smaller field sat down and shrank the
  creator's board. One relay from the joiner then started the clock with the
  creator about 28 m off their real e1. Fixed by judging the flags again when
  a seat's field is written.
- **Round 2.** That re-judge raised the flag of a player who had disconnected
  from e1, using their stale fix. That breaks decision 0037: the evidence ends
  with the socket.
- **Round 3.** Checking `connected` was not the fix, because connected is not
  the same as "sent a fix on this socket". A creator who left e1 and
  reconnected from home without saying anything could still be raised. Also,
  0061 and two comments described the code wrongly, so they were corrected
  before the decision became append-only.
- **Round 4.** Clean, once the re-judge only lowers flags. A flag is raised
  only by a `pos` or `ready` on the current socket, as in a same-field game.

The rule to keep: **anything that reshapes a board may lower a back-rank flag,
never raise one.**

- A same-field game is unchanged. `check-golden` against HEAD `4e0b42e` was
  identical in all 63 parts every round. Join answers were byte-identical
  across 113 answers (reviewer). Same-field screenshots under Playwright are
  pixel-identical inside the board, except the opponent's moving dot, which
  is caught at a different instant of its interpolation.
- Today's phone can play a remote game on two equal fields end to end. It
  cannot finish the handshake on a shrunk board, because it still draws the
  unshrunk field, and it shows no opponent dot because it ignores
  `opp_spot`. Both are 12.2.
- **The full suite flakes under machine load.** The worker tests took 2–4 s
  each, at the same speed on HEAD and on this code. With the default 5 s
  timeout about 190 tests time out, so runs used `--testTimeout=60000`. Even
  then, 3–9 tests in `carry`, `endings`, `record` and `watch` (pause,
  fallen-flag and wall-clock assertions) fail under full-suite load, and they
  fail the same way on HEAD. Every one passed when run alone.
