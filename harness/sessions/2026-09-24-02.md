## What was done

Phase 1 of a four-phase pipeline run (`harness/pipeline/ratchet.md`): **O-33**,
then **O-31** (the clock), then **O-30** (piece look plus a last-move
highlight), then **pinch zoom**. One commit carries this phase. Decision
**0043** supersedes the rest of 0023 and the accuracy clause of 0031.

- **A poor GPS fix no longer buys reach** (`src/shared/reach.ts`).
  `effectiveReachM(squareM, cfg, bonus)` is the dial plus the handicap, in
  squares, clamped. The accuracy argument is gone from it and from
  `inStartZone`/`walkToBackRankM`, so a caller that still thinks accuracy buys
  reach fails to compile. That covers the server's lift, place and ready checks,
  the client's circle, the handshake's "walk N m" and the board view. The hard
  refusal above ±25 m (`maxAccuracyM`) is kept; no constants changed.
- **Every out-of-reach refusal on a vague fix says the dot may be off.** The
  shared `outOfReachAdvice(walk, accuracyM, cfg)` adds "or if you are already
  there, your position is only accurate to ±N m…" above `goodAccuracyM`. It is
  used by lift and place and, after review, by the back-rank refusal.
- **`goodAccuracyM` stays on `ReachConfig`** as a label only (the GPS badge, and
  the cue above), because every game snapshots its config.
- **The rule applies to games in progress.** A carry lifted under the old,
  wider rule may be refused at place; Drop and lift again rescues it.
- **`scripts/analyse-survey.mjs`** modelled the rule from before 0031 (5 m plus
  accuracy). It now models the current one and reports accuracy refusals apart,
  so it does not advise raising reach for them. `README.md` and `gotchas.md`
  updated.
- **Tests:** unit tests and three DO tests (a ±14 m lift and a ±20 m place a
  square away are refused; the back-rank advice). 1016 → **1021**.
- **Drivers**, on `wrangler dev` :8811 from a fresh `--persist-to`:
  `drive-game`, `check-invite`, `check-clock`, `check-resume`, `check-record`,
  `check-review` all passed. A scratch pocketed-phone check also passed: reach
  reads 3.2 m at both ±3 m and ±20 m, a lift from a square away at ±20 m is
  refused with the new advice, ±30 m reads "too vague", and a good fix lifts.

### The review, in plain terms

**One round, clean.** The reviewer verified by running them that:
- no path still widens reach by accuracy;
- a carry lifted under the old rule and refused at place is rescued by Drop
  (tested in a scratch DO test);
- the back-rank handshake cannot strand a game.

Two of the reviewer's optional items were taken: the back-rank refusal got the
same "your position may be off" advice, and 0043 gained the sentence about Drop
as the remedy for an in-flight carry. The third, rewording the place-time
advice for in-flight carries, was **not** taken, because Drop covers it.

## What was learned

- **O-33 did not close O-12**, whatever O-33's text said. O-12 is the distance
  counter's floor, which guards against phantom metres; 0043 is about reach.
  O-12 stays open, now with a note saying so.
- **Square sizing never leaned on the bonus.** At 0.4 squares a fix may drift
  7.2 m before a player on the square is refused; the field walk's worst static
  scatter was 0.6 m. A good-signal game plays exactly as before.
- **Removing a parameter beats ignoring it.** The compiler found every caller,
  including two in the handshake that were easy to miss.
- **The survey analyser had quietly gone stale at 0031** and nobody noticed,
  because it runs only on real traces. A script that copies a rule rather than
  importing it needs its constants named and flagged ("keep the two in step").
