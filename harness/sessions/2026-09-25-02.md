- **O-48, the board opened scrolled off the top (`10.9.1`).** `swap` in

  `coordinateLabels` in `render.ts`, handles this. Zoomed in, each file letter
  and rank number goes in the same corner of the nearest cell of its file or
  rank that is on screen, at the same size. So it covers no more of a piece
  than at 1x, and nothing changes at 1x. Labels are deliberately not pinned to
  the canvas edge: a cell cut in half there has its piece drawn up to the
  edge, so a pinned label would sit on the piece. I checked screenshots from
  both seats in both piece looks.
- **O-45, a flaky test (`10.9.3`).** The test polls the presence row until the
  relay is stored. It passed 10 times alone and the whole file passed 10 times.
  I also found a second timing-dependent test: `record.test.ts`'s burst of
  refused places assumed two messages land under a second apart, and it failed
  once in six loaded full runs. It now measures its own window.
- **O-29, expired dev token (`10.9.4`).** It now reads "It has ended, and is
  never renewed."
- **O-25, deferred `ready` (`10.9.5`).** The 1 s ticker calls
  `considerReady(false)`. The latch is unchanged (O-26).
- **O-39, stale dot after a lift or place (`10.9.6`).** There is a new column,
  `presence.last_relay_at`, and the relay rate limit counts from it. The
  distance window still runs from `last_pos_at`.
- **O-36, clipped meters lost (`10.9.7`).** There is a new column,
  `presence.travel_owed_m`. What the sprint cap clips is paid under later
  windows' ceilings, up to 30 m (a sprint for one relay interval). It is

  paid after the result. This and O-39 had to go together: accepting the relay
  just after a move made the short-window clip common.

### The review, in plain terms

**Round 1, clean.** The reviewer ran these checks itself:
- **Schema 6 cannot resurrect a deleted game.** It probed archived and
  never-existing codes (peek, review, collect, socket), and all stayed empty.
- **A game in progress keeps its distance through the upgrade** (the
  "predates schema 6" test).
- **Owed meters cannot be banked.** They cannot be saved up while paused or
  before play, paid twice, paid after the result, or exceed what the phone
  reported. A random run of 200 reports credited 3413 m of the 4241 m
  reported.
- **No new inbound traffic.** O-39 adds at most one row write for each relay
  that used to be dropped.
- **The O-25 latch holds across ticks.**
- **The O-48 exception list is right.**

Taken from its optional list: a `schema.ts` comment called the new columns
"empty" when `travel_owed_m` defaults to 0.

Not taken:

- The short credit window right after a lift, for counter jumps over 30 m. It

## What was learned

- **A throttle and a rate cap can hide each other's bugs.** Dropping the relay
  after a move whole (O-39) was what kept its short window from clipping
  meters (O-36). Fixing one alone would have made the other worse.
- **A test that asserts "under a second" is a timing test.** Measure the window
  from stored instants, or pin it.
- **`check-record`'s residue now reads 0 m**, but that figure is floored by the
  carries, so it says nothing about the credit itself.
