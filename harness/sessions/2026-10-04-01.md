## What was done

This was phase 6 of run 2 of the pipeline (`harness/pipeline/ratchet.md`).
Done before it: the small fixes, O-44, O-21, O-50 and O-49, the latest live
as `b3946765` at `b09b892`. This phase was **8.3, the replay**. Then come
`8.5.1`–`8.5.3` (the share card) and `8.5.4` (the head-to-head record).

**This phase introduced the project's first stored location history.** Until
now the game kept one position per player, overwritten on every relay, plus
the lift and place fixes. One new decision, **0052**:

- **Each player's walk is kept** (`presence.track`, schema 8), built only
  from the `pos` relays and the lift and place fixes the server already
  receives. **No new message**, and no new row written: each fix is appended
  inside the `UPDATE` that already stores it (`GameDO.trackAppend`).
- **Board space only**, from the moment it arrives: squares from a1's centre
  to a hundredth of a square. No latitude is ever stored for it. Only while
  the game is `active`. At most 2,000 fixes a player.
- **Participants only.** It is in the seat-only review report and in the
  archive, and **never in the PGN** or any outbound game message. The archive
  has no corners and no bearing, as before.
- Each fix is `[tag, file, rank]`: the tag says which move it belongs to and
  whether a piece was in hand (`shared/track.ts`).
- **The archive stays `v1`**: `tracks` is optional, and an archive from before
  it reads back with `null`, so its replay draws each carry straight.
- **The replay** (`client/replay.ts`, `client/replay-draw.ts`, on the review
  screen): the board at every step, Back, Next, a scrubber, and a tap on any
  move in the list. Per step: the carry as an orange line from a ring (picked
  up) to a disc (put down), the mover's walk to the piece dashed, the other
  player dotted, earlier walks faint, and the words "Picked up standing on d8
  · Carried 41 m in 11 s · Put down standing on h4". The distance is the move
  list's own figure. Drawn on a unit board with no north arrow, by the game's
  own renderer: both piece looks and the pinch zoom. No chess.js on the
  phone; positions come from `applyMoveToFen`. **Scrubbing sends nothing**,
  so it works offline once the review has loaded.
- The **privacy statement** says the walk is kept, as squares, and is never
  in the file you share.
- **`scripts/check-replay.mjs`**: Fool's mate walked, then the replay
  scrubbed on the live game and again after the archive, the canvas checked
  for the ring and disc at the report's own fixes, zero requests offline,
  zoom, discs, and 404 for a stranger.
- **Tests:** 1276 → **1300**: `test/replay.test.ts` (property sweeps: the
  named square contains the point, positions match chess.js over random games
  with castling, en passant and promotion, the carry figure is the list's, the
  walk is partitioned exactly), archive codec, the worker track and the
  schema 8 upgrade.

### The review, in plain terms

**Round 1, FIX.** Privacy, budget, correctness and the schema all passed,
including a hand-built archive from before 0052, which replayed with straight
lines and the note, and a byte-identical PGN. The one must-fix was a flaky
driver: `check-replay` looked for the disc at the square's centre, and the
simulator's last fix can land a third of a square short. It now looks at the
report's own fixes and waits a fix interval after each walk; 6 runs of 6
passed. Also taken: `presence()` names its columns, so snapshots stop
copying the walk text, and the budget figure was corrected to up to ~78 KB a
game.

**Round 2, CLEAN.** The column list misses nothing; a probe showed the walk
surviving a pause, a disconnect, a re-join and a resume; the driver is not
tautological. A last check was added: the lift marker must be the hollow one
and the place the filled one, so swapping them fails.

## What was learned

- The relays already arriving were enough for a walk. The cost question was
  rows written, not requests, and riding in the existing `UPDATE` made it
  zero.
- SQLite's `DROP COLUMN` fails on the commented `presence` table ("incomplete
  input"). Production only adds columns; the upgrade test rebuilds the old
  table instead (`gotchas.md`).
- The RPC stub's types widen a tuple to an array; `index.ts` casts the
  report back.
