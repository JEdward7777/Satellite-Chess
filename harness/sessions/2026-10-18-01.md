## What was learned

- **A start script must record the process group it really started.** A
  `cd … && setsid npx … &` recorded the subshell's pid; the stop then missed,
  an early server kept the port, and one golden attempt ran against it. The
  fix: `setsid bash -c "cd … && exec npx …" &`, and check the port is free
  before starting.
- The 6-ply opening `g3h4 h6g5 h2g3 g5f4 g1h2 g7h6` gives Black a double jump
  from g3; a breadth-first search over the rules finds such lines in a second.
