## What was learned

**The naive distance count is off by four orders of magnitude, and the fix had to
be measured.** Summing consecutive fixes credits **19 km an hour** to a phone
sitting on a bench at ±3 m, and 32 km at ±5 m. Three separate mechanisms were
needed and each was worth about a factor of ten: smoothing five fixes before
looking at them, an anchor floor that scales with reported accuracy, and
requiring two consecutive fixes past that floor before paying out. The floor
factor of 2 is a real optimum rather than a midpoint — at 2.5 the count of a
genuine 672 m walk *drops* from 436 m to 288 m, because legs shorter than the
floor vanish entirely. Full table in decision 0020. **Do not "simplify" this back
to a sum.**

I had guessed the confirmation counter alone would be enough. It was not: 29.6 m
of phantom distance in five minutes. The reason is that the anchor is itself a
noisy point, so the gap between two fixes carries root-2 times the noise of one —
which I had not accounted for until the test failed.

**`@cloudflare/workers-types` shadows DOM globals in client code.** `document.body`
types as the Fetch API's `Body`, and `append` resolves to `HTMLRewriter`'s. Both
are compile errors that look like nonsense. `appendChild` and
`getElementsByTagName` are unambiguous and were the workaround. Logged as O-05 —
the real fix is separate tsconfigs for client and worker, and it wants doing
before phase 3 adds worker code alongside this.

**Building the client build before the views was the right call**, and worth
repeating. `1.6` was done ahead of `1.2` and `1.3` so that `--serve` plus `?sim=1`
could drive a real browser in the container. Every view bug found this session —
the caret being eaten out of the name box on each GPS fix, the status readout
wrapping at phone width, the compass "N" clipping off the canvas — was invisible
to tests and obvious in a screenshot.

**An arrow pad has to accumulate.** The first version measured each nudge from
the player's current position, so pressing an arrow repeatedly while already
walking went nowhere. Measuring from the current *destination* instead means
holding a direction covers ground, while pressing a different arrow still turns.
Found by a browser test failing for what looked like an unrelated reason.

**`ctx.serve()` returns `hosts`, not `host`**, in this esbuild version — the
banner printed `http://undefined:8788` until it was fixed.

## Loose ends not worth a stage

- **O-05** logged: the workers-types/DOM global collision described above.
- The distance count carries a fixed tail bias of roughly one floor plus the
  smoothing lag — about 20 m — at the *end* of a walk, because the last part-hop
  is still pending. Measured at 178 m counted for a 200 m jog. It is a constant,
  not a rate, so it is negligible over a game and not worth chasing. Noted here
  rather than as an observation because decision 0020 already covers the
  granularity trade it comes from.
- `?sim=1` currently starts both players at a hardcoded spot near Greenwich
  (`SIM_START` in `main.ts`). Fine for now; if a future stage wants the simulator
  to start on a *saved* field, that is where to change it.
