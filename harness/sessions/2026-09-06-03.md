## What was learned

The survey verdict, verbatim from `scripts/analyse-survey.mjs`:

```
Fix rate: median 1.0 s between fixes (p95 1.0 s, worst 81.4 s)
  ✓ Roughly 1 Hz, which is what the simulator and the budget assume.

## 1. Does reported accuracy mean anything?
  Claimed accuracy:  median 3.7 m, p95 4.2 m
  Actual error:      median 0.2 m, p95 0.4 m, worst 0.6 m
  Fixes landing inside their own claimed radius: 100%
  ✓ Reported accuracy tracks reality closely enough to build on.

## 2. Scatter while standing still — does the square flicker?
  three-minute static hold: median 0.2 m, p95 0.4 m, worst 0.6 m  (0% beyond half a square)
  one minute on B:          median 0.4 m, p95 0.7 m, worst 0.7 m  (0% beyond half a square)

## 3. Can the game be played on 8 m squares?
  A→B: measured 10.8 m against a paced 8 m  (error 2.8 m)
  A→C: measured 30.1 m against a paced 24 m (error 6.1 m)
   6 / 8 / 10 / 12 m squares: moves refused 0.0%, highlighted square wrong 0%
  ✓ 8 m squares play fine.

## 4. Repeatability — the error a board inherits for life
  Two readings of point A, 4.0 min apart: 5.8 m apart
  ! Consider re-calibration prompts, or averaging several fixes per corner.

## 5. Distance walked, cross-checked
  Two-minute walk: naive sum of fixes = 103.8 m
  Standing still afterwards: naive sum = 1.1 m → 0.1 km/hour of phantom distance
```

What this means for `1.9.3.5`:

- **The core assumption holds, with margin.** Consumer GPS resolved the ground
  far better than the simulator's noise model assumes — 0.2 m static scatter
  against a 4 m half-square. 8 m squares are safe; the analyser shows 0% refusals
  and 0% wrong-square down to 6 m. There is room to make the default square
  *smaller* than 8 m if a more compact field is wanted, though the repeatability
  finding below argues for caution.
- **Reported accuracy is pessimistic but honest.** The phone claimed ±3.7 m and
  delivered 0.2 m. 100% of fixes were inside their own circle. The reach rule
  (circle = f(accuracy)) is sound and, if anything, currently generous — worth
  noting when O-02's ceiling gets re-expressed as a multiple of square size.
- **The one real finding: 5.8 m calibration repeatability.** Two reads of the
  same stone four minutes apart differed by 5.8 m — an order of magnitude worse
  than the instantaneous scatter, because the receiver's bias wanders over
  minutes even when the noise is tight. A board calibrated from one fix per
  corner inherits ~this much offset for its whole life. `1.9.3.5` should make
  calibration **average several fixes per corner over a few seconds** rather than
  taking one, and/or add a re-calibration prompt. This is the concrete change
  the walk paid for.
- **Distance:** phantom distance while stationary was 0.1 km/h, nowhere near the
  19–32 km/h the simulator predicted the naive sum would produce — the
  accumulator's anti-drift constants have slack and could be relaxed. The
  2-minute walk's naive sum (103.8 m) still over-reads real ground covered, so
  decision 0020's mechanism is still needed, just not as aggressively tuned.
- **Pace-length caveat on section 3:** A→B measured 10.8 m for a "paced 8 m" and
  A→C 30.1 m for a "paced 24 m". Consistent ~1.3x over the paced estimate — most
  likely the operator's paces were longer than the assumed 0.8 m, not a GPS
  distance bias, since section 1 shows position error well under a metre. Don't
  read those as GPS distance error.
