### The review, in plain terms

**Round 1, FIX.** The leeway was right. The thresholds were not: they read
the claimed accuracy as a typical error, which called the owner's own phone
marginal on a default board. The 2026-09-06 walk (decision 0031) had every
fix inside its claim, a median claim of 3.37 m against 0.21 m of real error,
and no refusals on 6–12 m squares. Loosened to that data. Also, `check-field`
had been red since O-21 and was repaired.

**Round 2, FIX.** The verdict sentence contradicted itself through rounding:
the claim was shown whole and the leeway to a tenth, so "Looks playable"
could sit beside "±11 m … 10.8 m". The claim now goes through the same
formatter.

**Round 3, FIX.** Then through floating point: the leeway carries about
1e-11 m of projection error, so "±7.2 m … 7.2 m" read as "odd refusal", and
slack to hide it let a larger figure read as a yes. Fixed by construction:
the verdict is judged on the figures as displayed (`lengthFigure`). Round 4
swept 29.6 million cases and found no contradiction.

**Round 4, FIX.** A refused value read level with its limit: "±25 m, and
moves need ±25 m", "You are 3.2 m from e4 and your reach is 3.2 m". This was
in the real game's refusal text too, and predated this phase.

**Round 5, FIX.** The first fix returned "limit plus one step", which can
understate: 3.49 m against 3.04 m read "3.1 m", and the player steps once
and is refused again.

**Round 6, CLEAN.** The value itself is rounded up, with the limit as a
floor only. Three properties are enforced for every refused figure in both
units: shown ≥ true, shown > shown limit, within one display step (or the
floor). Six million random cases were clean.

## What was learned

- **Any number a verdict or a refusal stands on needs a property test from
  the first commit**: shown ≥ true, shown > shown limit, within one step.
  Four of six rounds here were one display bug each, every one found by a
  reviewer's sweep, and the bug was older than the phase.
- The claimed accuracy behaves as a ceiling, not a typical error, on the one
  phone measured. A rule built on the textbook 68% radius would have called
  every good field marginal.
- `wrangler dev` crashed twice under `check-units` ("Network connection
  lost" in the ProxyWorker) early in the phase, then never again in eight
  runs. Not explained; a fresh server cured it.
- The join race in `check-units`: home redraws when a field sync lands and
  wipes a typed code between the fill and the click.
