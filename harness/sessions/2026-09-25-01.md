## What was done

Phase 4, the last, of a four-phase pipeline run (`harness/pipeline/ratchet.md`):
O-33 (`7e3e1fd`), O-31 (`7d63f91`), O-30 (`3ab9e99`), then **pinch zoom**. One
commit carries this phase. New decision **0046**.

The owner asked for "a pinch zoom option". On a 5:1 field a piece is about 7 px
tall at 1x.

- **Zoom and pan (`10.8.1`).** Two fingers zoom about the pinch; one finger
  pans while zoomed; 1x to 6x. The zoom is a uniform scale and shift laid over
  the fitted projection (`client/board-zoom.ts`, pure and unit-tested), so a
  tap goes through the same `Projection` to a square as before. At 1x it is
  exactly the old view. The board cannot be panned off the canvas: its edge may
  come in by the whole board's own padding, and a board narrower than the
  canvas (a 5:1 field across its short way) stays centred. In memory, per
  view; nothing stored, nothing sent. `views/board-gestures.ts` wires it to the
  canvas and two buttons, on the game screen and the practice board (the same
  `drawBoard`, so both came for free). Other canvases are untouched.
- **A drag is never a tap (`10.8.2`).** A tap is one finger, alone for its
  whole life, never more than 10 px from where it landed, aimed where it
  landed. A pan, a pinch, a second finger, a cancel, a lost capture, or a lift
  the board never saw go down: never a tap. Anything that moves the view under
  a live finger ("Whole board", "Follow me", a canvas re-fit, the board turning
  round at the first snapshot) spends the touch but keeps the finger on
  record, so its lift still matches. A primary `pointerdown` clears fingers
  whose lift was lost, so the board cannot get stuck refusing taps.
- **No double-tap.** Two quick taps on one square are a lift and a put-back.
  The way back is a **"Whole board"** button over the corner, shown only while
  zoomed.
- **Follow (`10.8.3`).** A zoomed view holds still while your dot is clear of
  the outer 20% of the canvas and re-centres on you when you reach it. The
  first version shifted minimally and pinned a walking player to the edge they
  were walking towards (seen in a driver screenshot). A one-finger pan stops
  following, as does a pinch that leaves you off screen; "Follow me" and
  "Whole board" restart it. Never moves the view while a finger is down.
- **Drawing under zoom.** Things measured in meters scale (squares, pieces,
  reach and accuracy circles, destination dots, last-move tint, lifted-from
  dashes). The two players' dots, the north arrow, thin strokes and the labels
  (already capped at 14 px) stay at screen size. Strokes sized from a cell are
  capped at 6 px.
- **`touch-action: none`** on the canvas and on the buttons over it. The
  viewport meta is unchanged (O-47).
- **The simulator's drag** stands down while zoomed and for non-primary
  pointers.
- **Drivers.** New `scripts/check-zoom.mjs` drives real two-finger touch
  through `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`: pinch, pan starting on a reachable pawn
  (lifts nothing), tap to lift on a zoomed board, pinch while carrying, "Follow
  me", place, "Whole board", and Black the other way up being followed as it
  walks. `drive-game`, `check-review`, `check-clock` and `check-record` now tap
  with a `pointerdown`/`pointerup` pair marked `isPrimary: false`;
  `gotchas.md` says so.
- **Tests:** 1052 → **1088**.

### The reviews, in plain terms

**Round 1, FIX.** Verified safe: a pinch with one finger lifted early,
releasing a pan, a second finger mid-tap, `pointercancel`, a palm, follow
never moving the view mid-press, drawing consistent under zoom, and 1x
unchanged. Found: `reset()` forgot fingers that were still down. When Black's
board turned round at the first snapshot mid-pinch, the two finger lifts
became two taps through the turned board, which could be a move nobody made.
Fixed at the root: a lift the board never saw go down is never a tap.
`reset()` and `followMe()` spend live touches, and the four drivers now send
real down/up pairs.

**Round 2, clean.** No finger can get stuck on record (probed in a browser).
`isPrimary: false` in the drivers hides nothing. A real 1x tap in the sim still
teleports, as before. Taken from its optional list: a re-fit (rotation,
keyboard) under a live finger spends the touch, and `lostpointercapture`
cancels the finger.

### The run as a whole

Four phases in two days, each implemented by one agent and reviewed by a fresh
one until clean. All four came from the owner's first two outdoor games. Poor
signal no longer buys reach (0043). The clock never shows more time than the
player has (0044). Pieces are the standard set anyone can read (0045). A thin

round 1: an accidental move from a board turning under a pinch. None of it is
deployed. Each phase left a real-phone check (`10.5.4`–`10.8.4`), and STATE.md
now has them as one checklist for the next outdoor game.

## What was learned

- **A tap has to be proven, not assumed.** Every path that forgets a finger
  turns its lift into an unmatched `pointerup`. Treating those as taps "for the
  drivers" was the hole round 1 found. Change the drivers, not the rule.
- **Anything that moves the view under a finger has to spend the touch.**
  There were four such paths; the reviews found them one at a time.
- **Follow by page, not by nudge.** A minimal shift keeps a walking player at
  the edge of the screen, looking backwards.
- **The page keeps its scroll between screens** (O-48): a driver aiming at a
  measured canvas box silently tapped "Leave" until it scrolled to the top.
- **`pkill -f` with the server's command line kills the shell running it.**
  Start servers with `setsid` and kill the process group.
