**O-50, a game stuck on a broken field could not be ended or removed.** One
new decision, **0050**.

- **"End game…" on the board** (staging, active, suspended) opens resign,
  offer a draw, and abort. Anything that ends the game on the tap is asked
  about on a second screen whose button is dead for 1 s (`ENDING_ARM_MS`):
  resign, an abort alone, accepting an offer. "Keep playing" comes first.
  No `confirm()`. The opponent's offers show as a banner straight under the
  board; its Accept is dead for 1 s and then asks the same question. A
  declined or lapsed offer of mine is said in a notice. The model half is
  `client/endings.ts`; the rules are `shared/endings.ts`, which both ends use.
- **Resign** while active or suspended, whoever is connected, so the stuck
  player gets out alone. Refused while staging, pointing at abort.
- **Draw offer** stands until answered, the next move by either side, or the
  end; it survives a suspension.
- **Abort** (new inbound message `abort`, optional `accept`/`decline`;
  schema 7 adds `abort_offer_from`): alone while fewer than two plies have
  been played, handshake included; after that an offer, with the same lapse
  rules as a draw. Not in `waiting`.
- **An aborted game** has status `aborted`, `result_reason = 'aborted'`, no
  outcome. No record line (`pushRecord` sends only for `finished`), no
  archive, collected on unplayed terms moves or not (`collectionKind`),
  removable from "Your games", which says "aborted — no result" and offers
  the tidy-up as soon as one is listed. Its PGN, if the server is asked,
  says `Result "*"`, `Termination "abandoned"`, `SatelliteEnd "aborted"` and
  a closing comment. No review is offered.
- **A fallen flag is settled first** (`settleFallenFlag`): before lift, drop,
  place, pause, resign, draw and abort, the server makes the flag alarm's own
  check; if the running clock is out, the game ends on time and the message
  is refused. `suspendForDisconnect` ends the game on time instead of
  suspending when the flag has fallen. `finish()` does nothing to a game
  that is over.
- **A board that is over** hides Turn, On, Reach and the Invite button, and
  draws no reach and no square underfoot (`BoardView.over`).
- The tidy-up refusal message O-50 quoted now says how to end a game.
- **New driver `scripts/check-endings.mjs`**: the handshake abandoned and
  aborted alone, then tidied; mid-game with the opponent gone and the game

### The review, in plain terms

**Round 1, clean.** The reviewer walked every stuck shape (the handshake
never completed, the opponent gone mid-game, paused for a claim, waiting)
and found a solo exit for each and no unfair escape. An aborted game writes
no record row; schema 7 creates no object. Taken from its optional list:
settle a fallen flag before resign, draw and abort; a guard in `finish()`;
and a cleaner board once the game is over.

**Round 2, FIX.** Pause dodged a fallen flag: with the alarm late, pausing
banked 0 ms, and an abort alone before the second move then escaped a loss
on time. Fixed by settling before pause and inside the disconnect
suspension. The sweep also found an older gap: a move could be played, with
its increment, on a clock that had run out behind a late alarm. Lift, drop
and place now settle first; the walk a lift or place reports is credited
before the loss.

**Round 3, clean.** Only the server's clock decides, the walk is credited
before a loss on time, every timer ordering is right, and `over` is never on
for a live game. Taken: a retry in the driver's screenshot step (home
redraws under it), and DO tests for a place and a drop on a fallen flag.
