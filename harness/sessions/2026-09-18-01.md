## What was learned

1. **A relay that was sent is not a relay that counted.** The first version
   latched on `relayed === true`. The reviewer found how that strands a player:
   the server drops a `pos` inside `POS_SERVER_MIN_INTERVAL_MS` of `last_pos_at`,
   which a `ready` also resets. So a player refused one meter short who steps in
   within 1.5 s sends a relay that vanishes, and, standing still, never another.
   The fix gives an in-zone relay `RELAY_CONFIRM_MS` (3 s) for the flip snapshot
   to confirm it, and sends `ready` if none arrives. With that fix the driver
   could leave the relay cut on the cold-start page too, which means step 9
   proves `ready` itself did the resume.
2. **The Ready button had never worked visibly.** `onReady` sent `error` and then
   `broadcastState()`, and the client clears `lastError` on any good snapshot, so
   the refusal was wiped a millisecond after it arrived. Found only because the
   driver tapped the button, which every earlier test and driver had skipped.
   The general rule is now in `gotchas.md`.
3. **The positional start already worked via the relay (`onPos`), so auto-ready
   is a gap-filler, not the main path.** It matters in two cases: a player who
   stops inside the zone within 2 m of their last relay, and a status change
   (to staging, or a suspension) while someone is already standing still on
   their rank. The driver showed the first case happening naturally: with its
   relay live, black still needed one automatic `ready` on reaching e8.
4. **A stale `in_start_zone` was a real fairness bug**, not a tidiness one. A phone
   killed on its back rank kept the flag, so after reconnecting from anywhere it
   resumed the moment its opponent arrived.
5. **Clearing the flag at a *pause* was considered and rejected** (see 0037). The
   consequence: two players who pause while standing on their back ranks and then
   stay still will not resume until one of them moves or taps Ready. That is
   arguably right for a pause, and it is why the button stays visible even once
   the server agrees.
6. **Every older driver drops `=1` from `--base=…?sim=1`** (O-24), so none of them
   can be pointed at another port. `drive-game` and `check-clock` were run from
   scratch copies with the port edited in.
