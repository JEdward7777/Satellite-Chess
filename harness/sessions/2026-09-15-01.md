## What was learned

**A constraint written down once gets inherited until someone re-tests it — and
that just cost a second thing.** 2026-09-13 found three sessions of unrun browser
drivers behind "playwright is not installed here", and wrote the lesson down.
The very next session found the same shape guarding `2.1`: "cannot be finished in
a container", true when written, false on the machine the work is actually
happening on. Two instances is the point at which it is worth grepping the
harness for every sentence of that form rather than waiting for a third.

**The test-user note is the same failure with a worse ending.** It was recorded
once as a fact, propagated into `STATE.md` as a standing check, and the operator
had already settled it in conversation — but the settlement was never written
down, so only the wrong half was in the files. **Write down the resolution, not
just the concern.** A note that says "check X" with no note saying "X was
checked and is not a problem" will be re-raised for ever.
