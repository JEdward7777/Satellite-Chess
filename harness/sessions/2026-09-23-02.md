### What each review round found, in plain terms

1. **The carry that ends the game was never counted.** Distance only travelled on
   periodic position relays, so everything walked after the last one — the mating
   queen's whole carry — was lost from the headline, the PGN and the permanent
   record. Fixed at the root: lift and place now carry the phone's counter,
   credited through one shared function, before the move is judged (0041 rule 7).
2. **The screen contradicted itself, and the honesty sentence overstated the
   phone.** "You covered 43 m" sat above "60 m carrying". The carrying total came
   off the screen, and the sentence that claimed the phone misses "around a
   tenth" now says it counts short, more so with stopping and starting (O-38
   measured about a third in the simulator).
3. **On a short game the headline was below a single carry.** One move, then a
   flag fall: "You covered 0 m" above "1. e4 carried 16 m", in the review, the
   PGN and the record alike. Fixed at the source: the walked figure is the larger
   of what the phone counted and the player's summed carries — never the sum —
   in one helper used by the record, the report and the screen (0041 rule 8).
4. **Re-opening a finished game rewrote its distance.** A relay after the result
   stored its leg even while paying nothing, which turned a game nobody measured
   into the phone's whole page counter — on the review, in the PGN, and on the next
   re-join in the permanent record. **The owner's two real games of 2026-09-20 are
   exactly that case**, and would have been hit on the first re-open after the
   deploy. A finished game's distance is now frozen at the result (0041 rule 9).
5. **Clean.** The reviewer reproduced round 4's scenario end to end on its own
   `wrangler dev`, audited every write to the travel columns, and ran the suite
   three times. Three doc fixes were taken on its suggestion: 0041 rule 8 no longer
   claims old record rows are untouched (a re-push rewrites them; none exist in
   production yet), the PGN legend says the walked figure is floored by carries,
   and `pipeline.md` now requires a fresh, uniquely named `--persist-to` per server.

**Second opinions** were taken by the coordinating session before two calls: in

## What was learned

- **Every round found something a player would have seen**, and the implementer had
  reported green each time. Rounds 3 and 4 came from reviewers who built a game
  the drivers never play — one move and a flag fall; a finished game re-opened.
  The drivers only ever play Fool's mate to mate.
- **A number shown beside another number has to be derivable from it.** The screen
  put a phone-counted distance next to server-measured carries; nothing guaranteed
  their order, and the only fix that kept the screen, PGN and record in agreement
  was to make one bound the other where it is stored.
- **"Nothing is paid" is not "nothing is written."** `creditTravel` correctly paid
  nothing on a finished game and still moved its leg, which was enough to change
  what the game *meant*. Freeze state, not just credit.
- **The phone's counter is the bigger error now (O-38).** After rule 7 the server
  loses a steady 12–14 m of what the phone counts; the phone counts 58–72 m of a
  ~95 m Fool's mate walk, because the accumulator strands up to a hop at every stop.
  The floor hides this on stop-and-start games; it does not fix it.
- Test counts: 939 at the start of this session, **971** at the end.
