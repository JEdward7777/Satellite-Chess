### What changed

- **Home is ordered readout, Play, Your games, Your fields.** Play holds
  "Resume your game at <field>" for the most recently touched game in play
  (active or suspended; `resumeGame` in `views/games.ts`, one line, cut
  with an ellipsis if long), then New game, or for a player with no field
  the no-fields line and Calibrate, then Scan and the join box. Calibrate
  sits under the fields list once there is a field.
- **A GPS fix redraws only the readout** (the session notice, signal,
  accuracy, walked, a location error, in `data-home-live`). Home used to
  redraw whole on every fix, which emptied the join box about once a second.
  The full redraws left ("Show more", a field sync landing) carry the box's
  text, focus and caret across (`client/draft.ts`).
- **A field sync cannot drag a player back to home.** `showHome` awaits the
  game list; if another screen was mounted meanwhile and it is not home,
  it gives up (a screen counter in `swap`).
- **Long names wrap** (`overflow-wrap: anywhere` on both lists' names): an
  80-character unbroken name had widened the page to 1128 px at 320 wide.
- **`check-games` step 7** measures the last drawn element, not the scroll
  height, at phone sizes:
  - a seventh game costs no page against a sixth (390x664);
  - New game and Join are on the first screen of a 360x640 phone, above
    both lists;
  - home fits in two screens of a 390x844 phone, the height once scrolling
    has tucked the browser bars away.
- **More driver checks:**
  - Step 5b: a new account's Calibrate is on the 360x640 first screen.
  - Step 7b: a half-typed code keeps its text, focus and caret through three
    fixes and a "Show more".
  - Step 7c: both requests are held to force sync, then a tap on Calibrate,
    then the list, and the player stays on Calibrate.
  - `check-resume`: "Resume your game" is checked (named, one line, on the
    first screen), and the game is resumed through it.
- `check-games` takes `--base` as well as `--origin` and prints the server
  it drives. `lastTouched` is exported from `shared/game-index.ts` and
  shared.

## What was learned

- **A simulator-only layout reserve is part of every measurement a driver
  takes.** Measure the last drawn element, not `scrollHeight`
  (`gotchas.md`).
- **A bound that passes is not the same as a guard.** With the reserve taken
  out, the old bound would have let an uncapped list through. Assert the
  property itself (a seventh game costs nothing), and prove each check by
  putting the bug back.
- **A screen that redraws with `innerHTML` on a timer eats text boxes.**
  Redraw only what changes; carry a draft across what is left.
- **A screen that awaits before mounting must ask whether it is still
  wanted.** Two of home's bugs were older than this phase and invisible
  until review ran probes against them.
