## What was learned

- **Look before trusting the geometry.** The first placement, straight
  above the dot, covered e4 for a pawn walked from e2, which was exactly the
  destination being walked to. The first screenshot showed it and no test would
  have. The diagonal clears every neighbouring centre only when the carrier
  stands mid-square. Walking up and to the right it covers the square ahead
  until the dot arrives, which is accepted.
- **Anything drawn over the board is also a tap target.** The plate only
  painted, so a tap on it fell through to a square. Anything drawn over the
  board later needs the same hit test.
- **The existing snapshot is usually enough.** `carry` already carried the
  piece "so the opponent can see what is coming for them".
